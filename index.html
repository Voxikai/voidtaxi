How to ride

Pick your stop

Tap a pin or a row in the price list. Green means a warlock is parked there now.

Copy, then paste in game

Press Copy, then in game Enter Ctrl+V Enter.

Accept the invite

You get a Voidtaxi invite in seconds. Accept it, then the summon.

Pay on arrival

Pay the listed price once you land. Your summoner opens the trade.

Questions

Want a stop we don't cover yet?

Type the place, copy the line, paste it in game. The most requested spots get a warlock first, and we'll whisper you when yours is live.

/w Voidtaxi Booking vt: request ...

Nobody invited me

If no warlock is parked at your stop, Booking whispers you back with the stops that are open. You can also whisper vt to Voidtaxi Booking any time for the current list.

Is this allowed?

Yes. It's a player service paid in in-game gold only, like any summoning or portal seller in trade chat. We never take real money.

Can my guild partner with Voidtaxi?

Whisper Voidtaxi Booking in game or ask in the SumVentureCo Discord. Guild runs, raid nights and bulk bookings welcome.
