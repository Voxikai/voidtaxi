Nobody invited me

If no warlock is parked at your stop, Booking whispers you back with the stops that are open. You can also whisper vt to Voidtaxi Booking any time for the current list.

Is there an addon?

Yes, and it's the fastest way to ride. Install Voidtaxi on CurseForge, open your world map and click a stop pin to book in one click. Or click the Voidtaxi button (minimap or the floating one), pick a stop and press Summon me. Then accept the invite and the summon.

Online, Standby, Offline

Online: peak hours. We're fully open to everyone, and it's the best time to ride. Standby: off-peak. We're around but not fully open: riders at Honored reputation and above can book in the Voidtaxi addon, and we'll summon you if a warlock is free. Not guaranteed. Offline: nobody's on, check back at peak hours. The badge at the top of this page always shows which one we're in.

Sleeping Bag window

Sleeping Bag stops (the full chain and its 4 stops) book in the first 10 minutes of every hour (:00 to :10) while we're online. Why: they're 4 open-world stops, and every rider takes over 90 seconds of camping to log out and in. One bag run can hold up 15 to 20 other riders waiting in the same minutes, so we run bags together in one window and keep the line moving the rest of the hour. On Standby, Honored and above can book them any time in the addon.

Ranks: frequent flyer status

Your rank is your share of all Voidtaxi summons in the last 7 days. Ride a lot right now and you save right now: Dreadlord 1% of the week's summons = 25% off, Felguard 0.75% = 15% off, Succubus 0.5% = 10% off, Imp 0.3% = 5% off. Each summon counts for 7 days, like airline miles. Nothing is taken away; your rank shows how much you ride now. There's no limit on how many riders hold a rank. The prices in the addon already include it.

Reputation: your membership

Reputation is your long-term account with Voidtaxi. Every summon earns +500, Hated to Exalted, and it never expires. Each standing unlocks a perk you keep: Friendly priority in line when it's busy · Honored book during Standby (served if a warlock is free) · Revered 5% off every summon, added to your rank discount (a Revered Dreadlord saves 30%) · Exalted every 10th summon free. Rank rewards riding a lot now; reputation rewards riding with us over time.

Why speed matters

There are always more riders waiting: at peak, 20+ requests a minute, and every summon takes a warlock and two clickers. Accept the invite and the summon within 15 seconds each. Slower costs reputation; quick riders never lose a point. It's the only way we can keep the line moving for everyone.
